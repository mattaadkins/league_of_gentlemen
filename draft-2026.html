The draft opened on 10 August and the last pick landed on 18 August. It was scheduled for sixty picks and it stopped at fifty-three…

Graded against Sleeper's own board. Complaints go to Sleeper.

01The sixteenth pick

Before anything else gets graded, this needs dealing with.

Kelly drafted Tim Tebow
ROUND 2, PICK 16
For the record Tim Tebow is 34 years old and carries twelve years of listed experience. He was taken in the second round of a rookie draft, ahead of forty other players who are still playing.

Kelly followed it with Cole Kelley at 28 and a kicker at 40. One of his four picks was an actual rookie, and his class averages 27 years old.

04Reaches and steals

Kickers and defenses are left out, because Sleeper rates them generously and all twelve of you correctly ignored that.

BIGGEST REACH

Daniel takes Brenen Thompson at 18

The board had him 45th, which is twenty-seven slots in a draft that only ran to fifty-three picks.

BIGGEST STEAL

Creger takes Malik Willis at 47

The board had him 12th. Sam matched the same thirty-five slot swing two picks later with Mike Washington at 49.

THE OTHER REACHES

Sam +16, Matt +14, Glen +14

Skyler Bell went at 25 with the board at 41, Zachariah Branch at 12 with the board at 26, and Germie Bernard at 14 with the board at 28.

THE OTHER STEALS

Matt -31, Andrew -20

Keaton Mitchell went at 48 with the board at 17, and Braelon Allen at 33 with the board at 13. Andrew took the second-best value in the draft with four picks and no trades.

05Round four happened

Picks 38 through 44 went, in order, San Francisco defense, Anders Carlson, Jack Podlesny, Tanner Brown, Christopher Dunn, Trey Wolff, Matthew Trickett.

SEVEN CONSECUTIVE PICKS

No skill player was taken

A defense and then six straight kickers. Glen, Teague, Kelly, Justin, Daniel, Corey and Blue all took their turn.

THE DRAFT THEN STOPPED

Fifty-three of sixty

Round five produced five picks across eight days and the remaining seven were never made. Creger used two of the five.

06Whose picks were these

Fourteen of the picks had already changed hands before the draft opened.

MOST SWINGS

Blue, seven picks

He bought seconds off Matt, Corey and Kevin. He owns the best record in league history and has not won a title since 2017, so the accumulation is at least consistent.

FEWEST SWINGS

Matt, two picks

He sold his second to Blue and his third to Justin, then drafted twice. He is also the defending champion, which is the only defence available here.

BOUGHT LOW

Creger collected round five

He took Teague's fifth and Justin's fifth, and round five was where the two best values of the draft were still sitting.

DID NOT PARTICIPATE

Andrew, four picks, all his own

One trade in eleven seasons and none of them this year. He still finished with the second-best value in the draft.

08So that happened

Fifty-three picks were made across eight days, fourteen of them with borrowed capital, seven of them kickers and defenses in a single run. The best value in the draft went to the manager with the most titles, and the second pick of the second round went to Tim Tebow.
