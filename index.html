05Ninety trades

Forty-nine of them happened on MFL and forty-one on Sleeper. They are not distributed evenly.

THE MARKET

Kevin, 34 trades

He has been involved in more than a third of every trade this league has ever made. He has also never won a title and has lost two finals.

THE OTHER END

Andrew, one trade

One trade in eleven seasons. He is 74-67 over that span, which is the strongest argument anybody has ever made for doing nothing.

CHAMPIONS WHO DO NOT TRADE

Daniel, five trades, two titles

He has made five trades in eleven seasons and won in 2021 and 2023. Kevin has made twenty-nine more and won nothing.

THE BUSIEST CHAMPION

Blue, 28 trades

Second most in the league, and the last one that produced a title was in 2017.

09The trophy case

Nobody asked for these.

Best Team, Wrong Decade
Blue

He owns the best record, the most points and the biggest differential in eleven seasons, and he has not won a title since 2017.

The Only Sort That Counts
Creger

Three titles, more than anybody. He won the 2015 final at 7-5 and the 2024 final at 7-7, and he lost the 2016 final while his team was named Champion Of The World.

Do Not Disturb
Andrew

One trade in eleven seasons, a winning record, five playoff appearances and no finals. He has perfected doing the absolute minimum.

The Volume Discount
Kevin

Thirty-four trades, more than a third of every deal in league history, and two lost finals to show for it.

Peaked In The Group Chat
Corey

61-79-1 across eleven seasons, no finals, and 2-10 against Blue. He did tie a game once.

Won It And Left
Glen

He won the 2019 title and has gone 45-60 in the seven seasons since.

10So here we are

Eleven seasons produced six champions, and the two men with the best records between them have four titles and five lost finals. Two people have played every year without ever reaching a final. Kevin has made thirty-four trades chasing something Andrew got closer to by making one.
