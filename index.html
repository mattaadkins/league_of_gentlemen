So Here We Are

2026

Eleven seasons produced six champions, and the two men with the best records between them have four titles and five lost finals. Two people have played every year without ever reaching a final. Kevin has made thirty-four trades chasing something Andrew got closer to by making one.

This year Matt is playing for back-to-back and Creger is playing for the first three-in-five anybody has managed. Blue, who owns the only dynasty on the books, is playing for neither.
